# Battery Management Systems

A research documentation repository focused on **Battery Management Systems (BMS) in automotive applications**, with particular emphasis on **BMS cybersecurity threats, attack surfaces, vulnerabilities, and security controls**.

## 📌 Overview

This repository contains research on:

- Battery Management Systems in automotive applications
- BMS architecture and operation
- Communication protocols used in BMS
- Cybersecurity threats affecting automotive BMS
- BMS attack surfaces and potential weaknesses
- Security controls and mitigation strategies
- Automotive cybersecurity considerations
- BMS applications in high-performance and luxury vehicles

## 🔐 Cybersecurity Focus

The cybersecurity research examines threats across multiple areas, including:

- In-vehicle communication and network attacks
- Sensor and physical attacks
- Software and firmware attacks
- Algorithm manipulation
- External and ecosystem attacks
- Charging infrastructure
- Cloud and connected-vehicle threats
- Supply-chain risks
- Emerging automotive cybersecurity threats

The research also identifies relevant **security controls and mitigation measures** for the identified threats.

## 🎯 Research Objective

The objective of this research is to understand how Battery Management Systems operate within modern automotive environments and to identify the major cybersecurity risks associated with their increasing connectivity and integration with vehicle networks and external systems.

## 🔎 Research Areas

### Battery Management Systems

Research into the role, components, architecture, communication, and operation of BMS within automotive and electric-vehicle environments.

### Automotive Cybersecurity

Analysis of cybersecurity threats that can affect vehicles and their internal electronic systems, with a specific focus on BMS-related attack surfaces.

### Threats & Attack Surfaces

Identification and classification of potential attack vectors targeting BMS hardware, software, communication interfaces, sensors, external systems, and connected ecosystems.

### Security Controls

Research into security mechanisms and mitigation strategies that can be used to reduce the risk and impact of identified cybersecurity threats.

## 📚 Research Approach

The research was conducted using publicly available technical documentation, cybersecurity resources, automotive research, standards and regulatory material, and other publicly available sources.

The information has been consolidated and organized for **academic and educational purposes**.
